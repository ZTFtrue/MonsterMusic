<p><b>MonsterMusic</b> is an open-source, private, and customizable local music player for Android.</p><p><br><b>Key Features:</b></p><ul><li><b>Native C DSP Audio Engine:</b> Ultra-low latency audio processing powered by high-performance native C.</li><li><b>Dual Filter Architectures:</b> Choose between high-precision Biquad IIR filters and 2048-tap linear-phase FFT FIR filters with master stereo-linked soft limiting.</li><li><b>Studio Audio Effects:</b> Stereo Delay with ping-pong feedback, Reverb, Chorus, Flanger, Polyphony, and Stereo Widening (Spatial Virtualizer), each with dedicated Dry/Wet mix controls.</li><li><b>Audio Signal Chain Inspector:</b> View real-time audio pipeline details below the cover art (file decoder, sample rate resampler, DSP filter mode, and audio sink).</li><li><b>Frequency-Reactive Visualizers:</b> Dynamic Matrix Digital Rain and 32-band Audio Spectrum visualizers with frequency reactivity, fullscreen mode, and landscape orientation lock.</li><li><b>Pitch & Speed Controls:</b> Dual coarse (0.1x) and fine-tuning (0.01x) sliders for tempo and pitch stretching.</li><li><b>Interactive Synced Lyrics:</b> Smooth auto-scrolling, drag-to-seek timestamp navigation, and tap-to-lookup dictionary popup with multi-dictionary support.</li><li><b>Privacy First & 100% Offline:</b> Operates completely without network requests, no analytics, no ads, and no unnecessary permissions.</li></ul><p>More features and customizations are waiting for your discovery!</p>
